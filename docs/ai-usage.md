# AI use disclosure

I used an AI coding assistant during planning and implementation. It helped analyze the provided assignment, propose an architecture, draft portions of the C#, vanilla JavaScript, tests, and documentation, and identify additional boundary cases for review.

I directed the product scope and explicitly required the selected bonus operations, file/folder permutation coverage, the stale-source test, concise comments, the Quality Cube framing, and a walkthrough before recording. AI output was treated as a draft rather than evidence: changes were inspected locally and verified through compilation, automated tests, syntax checks, and prototype testing. Failures found during verification were diagnosed and corrected rather than hidden or bypassed.

No AI-generated claim substitutes for the repository's executable evidence. The [requirements matrix](requirements-traceability.md), [test plan](test-plan.md), source, and recorded verification results identify what the submission actually implements.
